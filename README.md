# 🎓 CampusBazaar — Verified Student-to-Student Campus Marketplace

> **The ultimate peer-to-peer student marketplace. Buy & sell used textbooks, exchange drafting & lab stationery, trade old scientific calculators, and connect directly with campus peers with zero middleman commissions.**

---

## 🌟 Overview & Mission

University textbooks and equipment are notoriously expensive. A single new semester can cost students upwards of ₹15,000 / $600 for books and technical gear that are typically discarded or forgotten once finals conclude.

**CampusBazaar** is an open, modern, and verified student-to-student marketplace designed specifically for university life. It facilitates:
1. **📚 Buy and Sell Used Books** (Textbooks, study guides, GATE/GRE/MCAT prep, semester notes).
2. **📐 Exchange Stationery** (Drafting sets, T-squares, molecular chemistry models, dissection boxes, and notebook bundles via 1-to-1 cashless bartering).
3. **🧮 Sell Old Calculators** (Casio fx-991EX, fx-82MS, fx-991CW, TI-84 Plus CE, TI-Nspire, and HP financial calculators).
4. **🎓 Student Listings** (Post any item in 60 seconds with live preview, campus pickup spots, and condition ratings).
5. **💬 Contact Sellers** (Direct WhatsApp integration, phone dialer, campus safety checklist, and live interactive in-app chat simulator).

## 🚀 Key Features & Highlights

### 1. 📚 Used Books & Course Notes Hub
- **Course Code Filtering**: Search by subjects like `MATH-201`, `CS-304`, `CHEM-201`, or `MED-101`.
- **Condition Grading**: Clear labels (*Like New*, *Very Good*, *Good*, *Annotated*).
- **Substantial Savings**: 60% to 80% below publisher retail prices.
- **Annotated Page Check**: Highlights, margin notes, and formula sheets noted transparently.

### 2. 📐 University Stationery, Pens & Drafting Hub (Sale & Barter Zone)
- **Drafting & Mechanical Pencils**: Full-metal Rotring 600 (0.5mm), Pentel GraphGear 1000 drafting pencil trio, and Staedtler Mars 780 2mm technical clutch pencils with rotary tub sharpeners.
- **University Exam & Lecture Pens**: Waterproof Uni-ball Eye (UB-150 Micro 0.5mm) anti-bleed exam pens, Pilot G2 comfort-grip lecture gel pens, and Sakura Pigma Micron archival ink fineliners for biology slide diagrams.
- **Highlighters & Specialty Rulers**: Stabilo Boss pastel study highlighter packs with index tabs, 12-inch triangular aluminum engineering scales (1:100 to 1:500), and Helix French curve sets.
- **Clinical & Lab Stationery**: 4-color medical charting pens + diagnostic pupil-gauge penlights for nursing/MBBS wards, plus spiral duplicate lab notebooks with tear-out carbonless yellow sheets.
- **Cashless Swapping & Giveaways**: 1-to-1 direct bartering plus 100% free exam hall starter kits for incoming freshers.

### 3. 🧮 Old Calculators & Tech
- **Exam Syllabus Legal**: Specific filters for non-programmable vs. graphic/AP approved calculators.
- **Battery & Case Inspection**: Verified solar cell, battery health, and protective slide covers included.
- **Flagship Models**: Casio fx-991EX ClassWiz, TI-84 Plus CE Color, TI-Nspire CX II CAS, HP 10bII+, Casio fx-82MS.

### 4. 🎓 "List an Item in 60 Seconds" (Student Listings)
- **Real-Time Live Card Preview**: Card updates live on the right as the student fills the form.
- **Deal Types**: Direct Cash Sale, Exchange/Barter Only, or 100% Free Senior Donation.
- **Pickup Spot Selector**: Choose between Central Library, Student Union, Cafeteria, or Dorm lounges.
- **Photo Upload & Preset Selector**: Upload local files with instant base64 preview or choose from preset photos.
- **Persistent Storage**: Saved in browser `localStorage` to persist across refreshes.

### 5. 💬 Direct Contact Sellers & Chat System
- **One-Tap WhatsApp Contact**: Auto-generates pre-composed messages with the item name, price, and campus location.
- **Direct Phone Dial**: Instant call trigger and copyable phone number.
- **In-App Interactive Chat**:
  - Live message thread.
  - One-tap quick inquiry buttons (*"Still available?"*, *"Meet at Library?"*, *"Test keys/condition?"*).
  - Instant simulated classmate replies to coordinate meetups.
- **Campus Safety Code**:
  - Daylight & public zones only (Library, cafeteria, security desk).
  - Physical inspection of keys and pages before UPI/cash payment.
  - Zero advance payments or scam links.

### 6. 🌐 Modern UX & Aesthetics
- **Currency Switcher**: Toggle seamlessly between **₹ INR** and **$ USD**.
- **Dark / Light Mode**: Eye-friendly themes with glassmorphism and modern university design accents.
- **Wishlist & Saved Drawer**: Bookmark favorite textbooks and track them in a sliding side drawer.
- **Campus Filter**: Filter specifically for Main Campus, Engineering Block, Medical College, Business School, or North Campus Hostels.

## 🏃 Getting Started & Running Locally

### Option 1: Direct Browser Launch
Simply double click `index.html` or open it with your favorite browser:
```bash
# In PowerShell:
Start-Process "index.html"
```

### Option 2: Run Built-In PowerShell Server
```powershell
.\server.ps1
```
Then navigate to: **`http://localhost:5500/`**
