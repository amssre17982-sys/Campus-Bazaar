Campus Safe-Trade Code of Conduct

Verified guidelines for 100% scam-free exchanges on university grounds.

1. Daylight & Public Spots Only

Meet in open, well-lit campus spaces with high foot traffic: Central Library Reference Section, Student Union Cafeteria, Departmental Atriums, or outside the Campus Security Office.

2. Test Before You Pay

For scientific calculators: turn on the screen, check contrast, run matrix equations, test all buttons, and check battery compartment for leaks. For books: inspect pages for tears or heavy ink markers.

3. In-Person Payment Only (UPI / Cash)

Never transfer advance token payments or scan random QR codes sent online. Only scan UPI or hand over cash after physical possession and inspection of the item.

4. Verify Student Credentials

Feel free to ask for their student ID card or verify their institutional email handle (@edu / @college.edu) for complete peace of mind.
